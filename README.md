# Matteo Lo Valvo

Music producer and developer based in Milan. On the engineering side I take apart mobile apps and undocumented APIs, build automation that runs at scale, and ship business software and e-commerce for small and mid-sized companies. On the music side I have been producing records in the Italian rap and pop scene for fifteen years, and still am.

[matteolovalvo.com](https://matteolovalvo.com) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matteolovalvo) · [matteo@lovalvo.it](mailto:matteo@lovalvo.it)

## What I do

**Reverse engineering**
- Android and iOS app analysis: APK decompilation and repackaging, TLS interception, certificate-pinning analysis, runtime instrumentation with Frida
- Private and undocumented APIs reimplemented as clean clients: authentication flows, request signing, protobuf payloads, mobile and web clients side by side

**Automation at scale**
- High-concurrency tooling in Go and Python: worker pools, proxy management, rate-limit and retry handling, resumable jobs
- Browser automation with Playwright and Selenium, Chrome extensions
- Android device automation and Kotlin utilities, Electron and smart-TV players, RTMP streaming
- Email and IMAP pipelines, data extraction and third-party integrations

**Product engineering**
- Business software: CRMs, booking and order management, invoicing, treasury and KPI dashboards, auction platforms, digital signage
- E-commerce: Shopify Online Store 2.0 themes, WordPress themes and plugins
- Websites and PWAs, test suites and CI on every project
