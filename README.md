# Matteo Lo Valvo

Developer and digital strategist based in Milan. I build business software, e-commerce storefronts and automation tools for small and mid-sized companies, after fifteen years producing records in the Italian rap and pop scene.

[matteolovalvo.com](https://matteolovalvo.com) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matteolovalvo) · [matteo@lovalvo.it](mailto:matteo@lovalvo.it)

## What I build

- **Business software**: CRMs, booking and order management, invoicing, treasury and KPI dashboards, real estate and auction platforms
- **E-commerce**: Shopify Online Store 2.0 themes, WordPress themes and plugins
- **Websites and PWAs**: fast marketing sites, multilingual sites with lightweight CMSs, installable web apps
- **Automation**: Python and Node.js tooling for email workflows, data extraction and third-party integrations
- **Other**: Android utilities in Kotlin, browser extensions, MetaTrader 5 expert advisors
