# Matteo Lo Valvo

Music producer and developer based in Milan. Fifteen years of records, growth strategy and code: I produce, mix and master for Italian rap and pop artists, co-run an independent electronic label, and build software for companies, from business platforms to e-commerce.

## Engineering

**Reverse engineering**
- Android and iOS app analysis: APK decompilation and repackaging, TLS interception, certificate-pinning analysis, runtime instrumentation with Frida
- Private and undocumented APIs reimplemented as clean clients: authentication flows, request signing, protobuf payloads, mobile and web clients side by side

**Automation at scale**
- High-concurrency tooling in Go and Python: worker pools, proxy management, rate-limit and retry handling, resumable jobs
- Browser automation with Playwright and Selenium, Chrome extensions
- Android device automation and Kotlin utilities, Electron and smart-TV players, RTMP streaming
- Email and IMAP pipelines, data extraction and third-party integrations

**Product engineering** (selected, private repositories)
- **Workshop management platform** for a car repair business: front desk, technician board, work orders, warehouse, courtesy cars, PWA with offline mode, 890 automated tests in CI
- **Real estate auction platform**: listings, live auctions with scheduled closing, KYC onboarding, Stripe payments, PHPUnit suite
- **Digital signage SaaS**: multi-tenant PHP/SQLite dashboard with Electron, LG webOS and Android player apps
- **Dental scan order management** PWA with role-based workflows for CAD designers and clinics
- **Members-only city guide** with Stripe and PayPal payments, editorial CMS and transactional email
- **Second-hand marketplace aggregator** searching eBay, Subito, Vinted and others, with alerts and a Telegram bot
- **Treasury and invoicing dashboard** with 2FA and webhooks; **construction job-costing** app; **livestock traceability** portal; several CRMs
- **E-commerce**: Shopify Online Store 2.0 themes (bundles, gift cards, Vitest-tested theme logic), WordPress themes and plugins; WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento and PrestaShop builds since 2017
- **Browser extension** adding BPM and musical key to Spotify, built in TypeScript with Vitest
- **matteolovalvo.com**: an interactive CV built as a working DAW in vanilla JavaScript, every sound synthesized live with the Web Audio API
